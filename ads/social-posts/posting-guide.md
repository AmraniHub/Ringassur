## Step 2 — Export the 10 post images

1. Open `posts.html` in Chrome (already served at http://localhost:3456/posts.html)
2. Set zoom to **100%** (Ctrl+0)
3. For each post, right-click → **Inspect** → click the `.post` div → right-click in DevTools → **Capture node screenshot**
4. Each image will be 1080×1080px and saved to your Downloads folder
5. Rename them: `post-1-prix.png`, `post-2-stat.png`, etc.

**OR** use any screenshot tool cropped to 1080×1080 on each card.

## Step 5 — Page Like Campaign (5€/jour, 14 jours)

### Structure
- **Type**: Traffic / Engagement campaign
- **Objective**: Page likes (Mentions J'aime la Page)
- **Budget**: 5€/jour CBO
- **Duration**: 14 jours (70€ total)
- **Expected result**: 200–500 followers à ce budget en France

### Targeting
- **Pays**: France
- **Âge**: 55–75 ans
- **Genre**: Tous
- **Audience**: Large (aucun intérêt — laissez Meta choisir)
- **Placements**: Facebook Feed + Instagram Feed uniquement (désactiver Reels/Stories pour Page Like)

### Creative for Page Like campaign
- **Image**: Post 9 (Social proof vert) ou Post 10 (CTA blanc)
- **Copy**: 
  > Rejoignez notre page pour des conseils mutuelle santé gratuits chaque semaine.
  > 
  > 👍 Suivez Ringassur pour ne rien manquer.
- **CTA button**: J'aime la Page

### How to launch (manual — Ads Manager)
1. Ads Manager → Create → Awareness → Page Likes
2. Ad Set: France, 55–75 ans, 5€/jour
3. Ad: Upload Post 9 ou Post 10 image
4. Copy above → CTA: "J'aime la Page"
5. Publish
